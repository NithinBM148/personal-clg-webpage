Faculty

The CSE-AI department boasts a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in various domains of artificial intelligence, including machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics. They are dedicated to delivering high-quality education and mentoring students in their academic and research pursuits.

Research and Development

The department actively engages in research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to participate in innovative projects. Research areas include machine learning algorithms, neural networks, AI ethics, autonomous systems, and more.

Facilities

The CSE-AI department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and facilities to support practical learning and research. These include labs for AI programming, data science, robotics, and high-performance computing.

Industry Collaboration

The department maintains strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from AI professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
