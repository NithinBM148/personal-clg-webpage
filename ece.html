Details

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) at Sri Venkateshwara College of Engineering (SVCE) is dedicated to providing high-quality education and fostering innovation in the field of electronics and communication. Established in [2001], the department has consistently evolved to meet the changing demands of the industry and academia.

Programs Offered

  • Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Electronics and Communication Engineering
  • Master of Technology (M.Tech) in VLSI Design
  • Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Embedded Systems

Faculty

The ECE department boasts a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in various domains of electronics and communication. They are committed to delivering quality education and mentoring students in their academic and research pursuits.

Research and Development

The department actively engages in research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to participate in innovative projects. Research areas include VLSI design, embedded systems, signal processing, wireless communication, and more.

Facilities

The ECE department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and facilities to support practical learning and research. These include labs for digital and analog electronics, microprocessors, communication systems, and VLSI design.

Industry Collaboration

The department maintains strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from industry professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
