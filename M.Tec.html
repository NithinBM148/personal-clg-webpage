Details

The Master of Technology (M.Tech) programs at Sri Venkateshwara College of Engineering (SVCE) are designed to provide advanced education and research opportunities in various engineering disciplines. These programs aim to equip students with specialized knowledge and skills to excel in their chosen fields.

Programs Offered

  • Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Thermal Engineering
  • Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Design Engineering

Faculty

The M.Tech programs are supported by a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in their respective fields. They are committed to delivering high-quality education and mentoring students in their academic and research pursuits.

Research and Development

The M.Tech programs emphasize research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to engage in innovative projects. Research areas vary based on the specialization, including advanced topics in thermal engineering, design methodologies, materials science, and more.

Facilities

SVCE is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and facilities to support practical learning and research for M.Tech students. These include specialized labs for thermal analysis, design simulation, materials testing, and other relevant technologies.

Industry Collaboration

The M.Tech programs maintain strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from industry professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
