Details

The Master of Business Administration (MBA) program at Sri Venkateshwara College of Engineering (SVCE) is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of business management principles and practices. The program aims to equip students with the skills necessary to excel in various business environments and leadership roles.

Programs Offered

  • Master of Business Administration (MBA) in General Management
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Marketing
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Finance
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Human Resource Management

Faculty

The MBA program is supported by a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in various domains of business management, including marketing, finance, human resources, operations, and strategy. They are committed to delivering high-quality education and mentoring students in their academic and professional pursuits.

Research and Development

The MBA program emphasizes research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to engage in innovative projects. Research areas include market analysis, financial modeling, organizational behavior, and strategic management.

Facilities

SVCE is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities to support practical learning and research for MBA students. These include computer labs, libraries, and collaborative workspaces designed to enhance the learning experience.

Industry Collaboration

The MBA program maintains strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from business professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
