Faculty

The Civil Engineering department boasts a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in various domains of civil engineering, including structural engineering, geotechnical engineering, transportation engineering, environmental engineering, and construction management. They are dedicated to delivering high-quality education and mentoring students in their academic and research pursuits.

Research and Development

The department actively engages in research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to participate in innovative projects. Research areas include sustainable construction materials, earthquake-resistant structures, water resource management, and urban infrastructure development.

Facilities

The Civil Engineering department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and facilities to support practical learning and research. These include labs for structural analysis, geotechnical testing, transportation systems, environmental engineering, and construction technology.

Industry Collaboration

The department maintains strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from civil engineering professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
