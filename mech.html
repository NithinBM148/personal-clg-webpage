Faculty

The Mechanical Engineering department boasts a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in various domains of mechanical engineering. They are committed to delivering quality education and mentoring students in their academic and research pursuits.

Research and Development

The department actively engages in research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to participate in innovative projects. Research areas include thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, manufacturing processes, robotics, and more.

Facilities

The Mechanical Engineering department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and facilities to support practical learning and research. These include labs for thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, materials science, and manufacturing.

Industry Collaboration

The department maintains strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from industry professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
