Faculty

The Mechatronics Engineering department boasts a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in various domains of mechatronics, including robotics, control systems, embedded systems, and automation. They are dedicated to delivering high-quality education and mentoring students in their academic and research pursuits.

Research and Development

The department actively engages in research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to participate in innovative projects. Research areas include robotics, intelligent control systems, sensor integration, and industrial automation.

Facilities

The Mechatronics Engineering department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and facilities to support practical learning and research. These include labs for robotics, control systems, embedded systems, and automation technologies.

Industry Collaboration

The department maintains strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from mechatronics professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
