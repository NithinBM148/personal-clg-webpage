Faculty

The Data Science department boasts a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in various domains of data science, including machine learning, big data analytics, statistical modeling, data visualization, and database management. Research and Development

The department actively engages in research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to participate in innovative projects. Research areas include predictive analytics, data mining, artificial intelligence, and data-driven decision making.

The Data Science department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and facilities to support practical learning and research. These include labs for big data processing, machine learning, data visualization, and cloud computing.

Industry Collaboration

The department maintains strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from data science professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
