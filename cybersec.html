Faculty

The Cybersecurity department boasts a team of experienced faculty members who are experts in various domains of cybersecurity, including network security, ethical hacking, cryptography, digital forensics, and information assurance. They are dedicated to delivering high-quality education and mentoring students in their academic and research pursuits.

Research and Development

The department actively engages in research and development activities, encouraging students and faculty to participate in innovative projects. Research areas include threat detection, vulnerability assessment, secure software development, and cyber defense strategies.

Facilities

The Cybersecurity department is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories and facilities to support practical learning and research. These include labs for network security, ethical hacking, digital forensics, and cybersecurity simulation environments.

Industry Collaboration

The department maintains strong ties with the industry, facilitating internships, workshops, and guest lectures from cybersecurity professionals. This collaboration helps students gain practical insights and enhances their employability.
